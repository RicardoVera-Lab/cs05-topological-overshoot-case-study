# Selected External Sources

This file lists the principal external publications used to frame and attack the CS05 question. It is intentionally a compact source map, not a systematic review.

## Primary phenomenon

Sokolov, A., Emersic, T., Nealey, P., de Pablo, J., & Snezhko, A. (2026). **Evidence of topological charge polarization at active-passive interfaces in acoustically powered active liquid crystals.** *Physical Review Research, 8*, 023060. DOI: `10.1103/nzfb-3cps`  
https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/nzfb-3cps

Role in CS05: source publication for the experimentally observed polarization/discharge phenomenon and the post-switch-off transient studied by this case.

## Same-platform elastic/director relaxation

Sokolov, A., Katuri, J., de Pablo, J. J., & Snezhko, A. (2025). **Synthetic Active Liquid Crystals Powered by Acoustic Waves.** *Advanced Materials, 37*(19), e2418846. DOI: `10.1002/adma.202418846`  
https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.202418846

Role in CS05: establishes rapid post-de-energization director/elastic relaxation in the same experimental platform, making stored elastic/director release a pre-existing rival rather than a newly invented explanation.

## Related defect-transport model

Tavera-Vázquez, A., et al. (2026). **Spatial Activity Patterning and Topological Defect Transport in Acoustically Energized Active Liquid Crystals.** *Small*. DOI: `10.1002/smll.74378`  
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/smll.74378

Role in CS05: provides a neighboring minimal defect-transport description and a strong conventional null family for charge-dependent motion.

## Activity-gradient control of defects

Shankar, S., Scharrer, L. V. D., Bowick, M. J., & Marchetti, M. C. (2024). **Design rules for controlling active topological defects.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 121*, e2400933121.  
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11127047/

Role in CS05: constrains broad novelty claims around charge-selective defect response to activity gradients.

## Active/passive defect transport

Chaithanya, K. V. S., Ardaševa, A., Meacock, O. J., Durham, W. M., Thampi, S. P., & Doostmohammadi, A. (2024). **Transport of topological defects in a biphasic mixture of active and passive nematic fluids.** *Communications Physics, 7*, 302.  
https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-024-01792-6

Role in CS05: constrains broad claims around defect transport and charge segregation across active/passive environments.
