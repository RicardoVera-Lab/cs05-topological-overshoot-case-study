# Public Disclosure Boundary

## Public by design

This repository exposes only what is needed to evaluate CS05 as a public research artifact:

- research question;
- competing hypotheses;
- preregistration;
- public analysis code;
- synthetic validation;
- reproducibility material;
- hashes;
- claim boundaries;
- final status.

## Proprietary by design

CIEC LAB internal operating methods are not disclosed.

No operational architecture is required to evaluate the public claims in this repository.

## Principle

**Public evidence. Private machinery.**
