# Proprietary Research System Notice

This project used internal research systems named:

- ALETHEIA
- OSINTEGA
- FORGE
- INVENTOR
- COMPUTE

Only these names are disclosed in the public repository.

The following remain proprietary and are intentionally not published:

- prompts and master specifications;
- role definitions and functional assignments;
- sequencing and routing logic;
- decision authority and governance rules;
- internal thresholds and promotion/termination logic;
- handoff formats and internal research records;
- implementation details of the laboratory architecture.

The public repository is designed to make the **scientific case and its reproducibility** auditable without disclosing the private laboratory system that organized the research.
