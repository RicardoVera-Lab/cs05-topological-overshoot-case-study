# Research Question

## Observed anomaly

A published experiment in acoustically powered active liquid crystals reported that, after activity is switched off, the magnitude of topological polarization briefly increases before ordinary discharge proceeds.

## CS05 question

> **What quantitatively causes the post-switch-off polarization overshoot?**

The project deliberately avoided treating the anomaly itself as a discovery.

## Rival hypotheses

### H0 — Passive defect-relaxation null
The defect configuration present at switch-off is sufficient for standard post-drive interactions, annihilation, and transport to generate the overshoot.

### H1 — Stored elastic/director release
Residual elastic/director stress released after switch-off adds a transient charge-selective current beyond the passive defect-level null.

### H2 — Measurement/accounting artifact
Smoothing, field boundaries, birth/death bookkeeping, track loss, or charge assignment materially create or amplify the overshoot.

## Required evidence

The first discriminating step required charge-resolved defect trajectories rather than a new mechanistic model.
