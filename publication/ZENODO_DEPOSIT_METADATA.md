## Description
Independent, human-directed and AI-assisted technical research report investigating a published post-switch-off topological-polarization overshoot in an acoustically powered active liquid crystal. The project narrowed the question through prior-art analysis, retained three rival explanations, preregistered a charge-resolved accounting experiment, froze the analysis implementation before access to the desired real trajectories, and validated the software against adversarial synthetic cases. The decisive real-data experiment was not executed because the required external charge-resolved trajectory dataset was not obtained at the evidentiary quality specified in the preregistration. Final outcome: **Unresolved due to unavailable external raw data. No mechanism claim made.**
